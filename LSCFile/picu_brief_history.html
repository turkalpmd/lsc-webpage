Pediatric Intensive Care

Brief History

Key milestones in the development of pediatric intensive care at Hacettepe University, from establishment to the present.

About Us Administration Fellows Thesis SMR Album Brief History Testimonials
  • 1985
    Establishment
    The first-ever PICU of Türkiye was established within the Hacettepe University İhsan Doğramacı Children's Hospital with a six-bed capacity.
    • Prof. Namık Çevik cut the ribbon being the Director of the Children's Hospital.
    • A closed-model ICU with a 1:2 nurse-to-patient ratio was led by the chief residents under the operational directive of Dr. Faik Sarıalioğlu.
    • Academic output began on day one — a mumps myocarditis case was published in the international literature (Jpn Heart J 1989).
  • 1997
    Renovation
    Renovated as a 10-bed ICU with the addition of an isolation room.
    • Prof. Murat Tuncer executed the renovation as the Director of the Children's Hospital.
  • 2001
    Mission
    Pediatric intensive care ecole transference to Hacettepe University.
    • Benan Bayrakci was appointed at Johns Hopkins University for this purpose.
    • Prof. Gülsev Kale pursued the mission as the Chair of Pediatrics.
  • 2002
    Closed System ICU
    Transformation to Closed System ICU format.
    • Benan Bayrakci restructured the management of the unit being the Head of PICU.
    • Habilitated unit with invasive monitorization, mechanic ventilation and blood purification applications.
  • 2003
    Home Ventilation
    Home ventilation service initiated.
    • Benan Bayrakci started up the program being the pioneering service in Türkiye.
  • 2004
    CVVHDF
    CVVHDF Program initiated.
    • Benan Bayrakci started up the program as the Head of PICU.
  • 2006
    PALS
    PALS program started.
    • Assoc. Prof. Benan Bayrakci established the program as the Head of PICU.
  • 2007
    ECMO Center
    Established as the first ever ECMO program of Türkiye.
    • Assoc. Prof. Benan Bayrakci founded the center being the Head of PICU, following his training at Johns Hopkins University.
    • Prof. Tezer Kutluk ensured the facility as the Director of the Children's Hospital.
    • The ECMO cohort now approaches 400 patients with more than 600 ECMO days provided annually.
  • 2007
    National Congress & First AHA PALS Course
    The National Congress of Pediatric Emergency Medicine and Intensive Care was hosted at Hacettepe University with the participation of internationally recognized authorities.
    • The first American Heart Association (AHA)–certified Pediatric Advanced Life Support (PALS) Provider Course in Türkiye was organized during the same meeting.
    • This established an internationally accredited standard for pediatric resuscitation training in the country.
  • 2009
    Subspecialty Recognition
    Pediatric intensive care was accepted as a pediatric subspecialty field in Türkiye through the TUKMOS studies led by Dr. Bayrakcı.
  • 2010
    Division & Training Program
    The Hacettepe University Division of Pediatric Intensive Care was formally established.
    • Associate Prof. Benan Bayrakcı was appointed founding division chief.
    • Türkiye’s first Pediatric Intensive Care Subspecialty Training Program was initiated.
  • 2011
    PAYlaşım Meetings
    Annual “PAYlaşım” meetings were launched to disseminate knowledge and experience across the national PICU community.
  • 2013
    Modern Facility
    The unit relocated to a technologically advanced 16-bed facility on the 5th floor, 3rd block.
    • Clinical outcomes improved measurably; SMR declined to as low as 0.28 and has remained at this level.
    • The first Joint Pediatric Intensive Care Training Program in Türkiye began (2013–2019) with Ankara Children’s Hematology Oncology Hospital.
  • 2014
    First Fellow Graduate
    Dr. Selman Kesici became the first graduate of a pediatric intensive care fellowship program in Türkiye.
  • 2015
    Life Support Center (LSC)
    The Life Support Application and Research Center was established as a platform for research, simulation-based education, and novel therapeutic development.
    • Founded by Prof. Benan Bayrakcı.
  • 2018
    pCICU & Nursing Program
    Academic staff assumed responsibility for the 8-bed pediatric cardiac intensive care unit (pCICU).
    • Certified Pediatric Intensive Care Nursing Training Program launched with the Turkish Ministry of Health.
    • Dr. Selman Kesici returned as faculty to train future specialists.
  • 2024
    Life Support Trust Foundation
    The Life Support Trust Foundation was established to sustain scientific research and provide structured social support for patients and families.
  • 2025
    40th Anniversary Congress
    Four decades of pediatric intensive care were commemorated with the 40th Anniversary Congress, organized with the participation of leading international authorities.
    • The idea of documenting this historical trajectory as a guiding framework for newly established intensive care units was conceived during the meeting.
  • Currently
    Quaternary Referral Center
    The division functions as a national quaternary referral center with 23 intensive care beds (16 PICU + 8 pCICU), integrating ECMO, transplantation, intoxications, blood purification, and trauma care with education and research.
    • Coordinated by Prof. Benan Bayrakcı, Assoc. Prof. Selman Kesici, and Assoc. Prof. Mehmet Çeleğen.
    • ECMO cohort approaches 400 patients with more than 600 ECMO days annually.
Translational Work

Innovations & Protocols

Innovative therapeutic and translational approaches developed within the unit and introduced into the scientific literature:

  • Zipper Method of Hacettepe

    A novel immunomodulation technique for severe neurological inflammatory disorders.

  • PISA Protocol

    A treatment protocol for severe COVID-19-associated MIS-C.

  • SeLBA Method

    Selective left bronchial aspiration for the recruitment of left lung atelectasis.

  • Rapid Sequence Apheresis

    An apheresis technique for poisoning treatment.

  • ECMO-supported MSC Therapy

    Mesenchymal stem cell therapy for hypoplastic lung disease.

  • Hyperbaric Organ Preservation

    Hyperbaric oxygen preservation of donor organs prior to transplantation.

  • Carbon Dioxide Apnea Test

    The Hacettepe CO2-based apnea test procedure in ECMO patients.

  • Dialoxygenation

    The use of dialysis membranes as oxygenators.
