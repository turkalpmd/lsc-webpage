Research and education in the PICU

Dr. Çeleğen is the principal investigator of the PICU Research Committee. Studies carried out in the Life Support Center laboratories and the training programs of the Pediatric Intensive Care Unit run under his coordination. Each unit keeps its own crew; he brings their research agenda, fellow projects and teaching together.

Laboratories

  • Xenotransplantation Lab Cross-species organ support
  • Hybrid Membranes Lab Combined dialysis and oxygenation
  • Digital Health & AI in Critical Care Data science for the critically ill child
  • Nasal High-Flow Oxygenation Non-invasive respiratory support
  • Extracorporeal Circuit Animal Lab Low-flow CO₂ removal
  • All laboratories Including industry partners
