Not for de-escalation or modulator allocation

The score is meant to prompt low-burden measures in modulator-untreated patients: earlier review, adherence and airway-clearance assessment, culture surveillance. A low score is not a reason to reduce follow-up below guideline standards, and the score does not decide who receives a modulator.

Missing inputs

FEV1 z-score and IV-antibiotic days are required; without them no risk is shown. Unknown BMI z-score is replaced by the development median (−0.41) and the risk is shown as a range for BMI z-scores of +1 to −2. Unknown culture results are scored as negative, so the risk is a lower bound.

How it was built

Logistic regression of 1,122 prediction-years from 539 patients in 18 centres (251 IV-treated exacerbations). IV-antibiotic days enter as ln(1 + days), so the first course adds the most points. Item points are rounded for display and may not add up exactly to the total.

Use it with care

Validated in patients not used to build it, in 2024 and across centres of one registry; external validation is still needed. The overall risk level differed between centres, so check the score against your own event rate.

Everything is calculated in this page. No data are stored or sent. The equation, the trained machine-learning model and the analysis code are in the study repository.
